🎲 Game Arcade 🎯

How to play

Modes

  • 2 players: take turns on the same screen. vs Computer: you play first. Sudoku, Mines and Pinball are solo (Sudoku also has 2 players).
  • Online rooms are not available on this static Netlify build. Same-device play and computer opponents work without an account.
  • Difficulty (Easy to Expert) changes the computer's strength, Sudoku clues, Mines board size and Pinball speed. Changing it starts a new game.

Xiangqi (Chinese chess)

  • Red moves first. Tap a piece, then tap a dot to move. Win by checkmate, or when the other side has no legal move.
  • General (帥/將) one step in the palace, and the two generals may never face each other on an open file. Advisor (仕/士) one diagonal step in the palace.
  • Elephant (相/象) two diagonal steps, never across the river, blocked if the middle point is occupied. Horse (馬) one step straight then one diagonal, blocked by a piece next to it.
  • Chariot (車) any distance in a straight line. Cannon (炮) moves like a chariot but captures by jumping exactly one piece. Soldier (兵/卒) one step forward, and sideways after crossing the river.
  • Blind variant: pick "Blind" in the variant menu and press New game. Every piece except the generals starts face-down and shuffled over all positions. A face-down piece moves like the piece whose spot it stands on, then flips. After flipping it uses its true identity and colour, so a piece of the other colour can end up on your side and then belongs to your opponent. Flipped advisors and elephants may leave the palace but never cross the river.

Go

  • Black plays first. Tap an intersection to place a stone. A stone or group with no empty neighbours is captured. You may not play a suicide move or immediately repeat the previous position (ko).
  • Press Pass when you have nothing useful to play. After two passes, tap dead stones to remove them and press Finish & score. Area scoring, White gets 7.5 komi. Pick 9, 13 or 19 lines in the menu.

Chess

  • White moves first. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square. Castling, en passant and promotion (always to a queen) are included. Checkmate wins, stalemate is a draw.

Banqi (half-board)

  • All 32 pieces start face-down on a 4×8 board. On your turn either flip one face-down piece, or move one of your face-up pieces one step (up, down, left, right) onto an empty square or onto an enemy piece it can capture. The first piece Player 1 flips decides their colour.
  • Rank: General > Advisor > Elephant > Chariot > Horse > Cannon > Soldier. A piece captures an equal or lower rank. Exceptions: a Soldier can capture a General, but a General cannot capture a Soldier. A Cannon captures by jumping exactly one piece in a straight line. Face-down pieces cannot be captured.
  • You win by capturing all enemy pieces or leaving your opponent with no move.

🧩 Sudoku

  • Fill the grid so each row, column and 3×3 box contains 1 to 9 once. Tap a square, then a number (or type it). Clashes turn red.
  • 2 players (same screen): take turns placing one number each. A correct number scores +1, a wrong one costs 1. The most points wins.

💣 Mines

  • Tap a square to dig. A number tells how many mines touch that square. Switch the button above the grid to Flag (or right-click) to mark mines. Your first tap is always safe. Clear every square without a mine to win.

🔮 Pinball

  • Hold Space (or press and hold the screen) to pull the plunger, release to launch. Flip with the arrow keys (or Z and /), or press the left and right halves of the screen.
  • Scoring: bumpers 100, each star 500 (clear all three for 5000 and a higher multiplier), lighting the three top lanes 3000, the purple ramp 2000, the black hole 1500. Losing a ball resets the multiplier.
